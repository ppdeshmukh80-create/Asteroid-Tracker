About the data

Close approaches

A close approach means an object passes relatively near Earth. It does not mean the object is predicted to hit Earth.

Measurements

Miss distance is the separation between Earth and the object at closest approach. Relative velocity is their speed relative to each other at that time.

Source and snapshot

Data source: NASA/JPL Small-Body Database Close Approach Data API.
